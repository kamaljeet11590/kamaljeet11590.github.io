The idea

Building is easy once you know what to build. Scaling is where the effort goes.

Most AI courses stop at the demo. This one starts there and keeps going: what to build and why, how to make it work, and what it takes to run it for real users at a cost you can defend.

One real product

Taught on a working desk

StockTickers Mini, a stock research desk with chat, agents, retrieval, evaluations and an open model served on a GPU, is the example in every module. You see each idea running, not drawn.

Measured, not guessed

Numbers that decide

Latency, token cost, failure rates and capacity are read from the running product, and every outside claim is cited. Slides carry tables, diagrams and worked numbers, not bullet lists.

Beginner to expert

Every module climbs

Each module starts with the vocabulary and ends with the trade-offs a senior engineer defends in a design review: capacity models, error budgets, threat models, unit economics.
